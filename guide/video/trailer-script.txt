SUPPLY RUN TO BRAVO - 30-SECOND TRAILER
Narration script for voiceover (adult team members only)
Team: Swinging Monkeys - NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
=====================================================================

The words below are burned into the video as captions. Each line has a
time window: start reading when the caption appears and finish before
it fades. About 72 words in 30 seconds, so read at a lively but clear
pace (roughly 2.5 words per second). Short pauses between scenes are fine.

Suggested casting: Voice A (mission narrator) and Voice B (mission
control). One person can read everything if that is easier.
Space Apps rule: only adults may record. No voices of anyone under 18.

MIXING NOTES
  - The video ships with an original music bed only (no voice yet),
    peaking around -6 dBFS (about -20 LUFS).
  - When you add the voiceover, duck the music by about 8 to 10 dB
    under the voice, and aim for about -16 LUFS for the final mix.
  - Timed captions in WebVTT format: trailer-captions.vtt (same folder).
  - Record at 48 kHz in a quiet room; leave 0.5 s of silence at the start.
